Pamukkale Hot Springs

Terraces of mineral-rich hot springs cascading down white travertine cliffs.

Region

Pamukkale, Denizli

Activity Type

Thermal Terraces

Duration

Half-Day
Why Visit
01

Pamukkale means "cotton castle" in Turkish, named for the brilliant white travertine terraces formed by centuries of mineral-rich water. It is one of Turkey's most recognisable natural sites and a short trip from several partner clinics.

What to Expect
01

You will walk the white terraces barefoot, as required to protect them, and soak in the warm thermal pools along the way, with sweeping views across the valley below.
