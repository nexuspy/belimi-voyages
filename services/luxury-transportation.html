About the Service

Getting to and from appointments in an unfamiliar city adds stress you do not need after a procedure. Belimi Voyages arranges private limousine pickup from the airport and transfers between your hotel and the clinic for every appointment, so travel is one less thing to plan.

Key Deliverables

What is included in the luxury transportation service.

Airport Pickup

Private limousine pickup on arrival and drop-off for departure.

Hotel-Clinic Transfers

Scheduled transport between your hotel and the clinic for every appointment.

Local Driver Support

A driver familiar with the clinic and city, on call for your schedule.

Flexible Scheduling

Transfers arranged around your treatment timeline, not a fixed itinerary.
