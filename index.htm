What treatments do you offer?

Dental treatment, hair implants, and facial surgery, each paired with vetted clinics, transfers, and accommodation in Turkey.

How Are Clinics Vetted?

Every clinic and partner is personally vetted before being offered to a client. That is why we work with a small, trusted network rather than a large, unchecked one.

What Is Included In A Package?

Airport pickup, hotel-to-clinic transport, accommodation and meals, plus optional sightseeing in Cappadocia, Pamukkale, or Antalya.

What Languages Do You Support?

French, English, and Czech. The founder is personally involved in every partner relationship, in whichever language works for you.

How Does Pricing Work?

There is no fixed package price. We walk you through clinic and treatment costs transparently before you commit to anything.

How Do I Get Started?

Message us on WhatsApp or the contact form with your treatment goals. We will match you with a vetted clinic and handle the logistics.
